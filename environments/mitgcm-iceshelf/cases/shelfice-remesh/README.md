# shelfice-remesh

Upstream test: `code/mitgcm/verification/shelfice_2d_remesh/input`. Policy: `pointwise`.

## The test

Two-dimensional cavity with vertical remeshing of the top grid cell. `run.sh` builds one MITgcm executable for this
configuration with the tree's own `tools/genmake2` (the build configuration
under `mods/`: `SIZE.h`, `packages.conf` and the option headers of the
upstream experiment, gfortran optfile `linux_amd64_gfortran`, one process,
tiles only), then runs it on the deck under `ic/<ic>/`. Configuration:
verification/shelfice_2d_remesh/input: a 1x200x90 meridional slice of a southern-ocean shelf (spherical-polar, 0.0078125 deg in latitude, 90 uniform 10 m levels, four 1x50 tiles, one process) with a flat 900 m bottom, an open northern boundary prescribing v, theta and salt with a linear sponge, and a trapezoidal ice shelf whose mass evolves under SHELFICEMassStepping driven both by its own melt and by a deliberately unrealistic prescribed tendency shelfice_dMdt.r02.bin of +/-0.0286 kg/m2/s, so that the top grid cell crosses the merge threshold of 0.10 and the split threshold of 1.12 several times in a short run; ALLOW_SHELFICE_REMESHING is compiled in from the experiment's own SHELFICE_OPTIONS.h, remeshing is attempted every SHELFICEremeshFrequency=600 s (every two steps), the melt uses the three-equation formulation with gamma friction (SHELFICEuseGammaFrict=.TRUE., SHELFICEselectDragQuadr=2, shiCdrag=SHELFICEDragQuadratic=0.0015) and no boundary layer, the free surface is non-linear with real fresh-water flux, vertical viscosity and diffusivity are implicit and enhanced under thin top cells (pCellMix_select=20), convection is by ivdc_kappa=1, and momentum uses the vector-invariant form with 77-scheme advection of theta and salt; the run restarts from the deck's pickup and pickup_shelfice at iteration 2898 and the graded window is 80 steps of 300 s (6.7 hours, four times the deck's 20 steps), which should produce of the order of sixteen remeshing events instead of four..

The production path it forces: pkg/shelfice/shelfice_remeshing.F together with shelfice_remesh_state.F, shelfice_remesh_c_mask.F, shelfice_remesh_uv_mask.F and shelfice_remesh_calc_w.F, which every two steps test the top-cell thickness factor against the split and merge thresholds and, where one is crossed, move the ocean surface up or down a level and redistribute theta, salt, momentum and the free-surface anomaly so that heat, salt and volume are conserved; pkg/shelfice/shelfice_step_icemass.F and shelfice_thermodynamics.F with the gamma-friction transfer coefficients; model/src/cg2d.F at about 115 iterations per step to a target of 1e-11; the non-linear free-surface rescaling in model/src/calc_surf_dr.F and update_surf_dr.F, which is exercised hard because the top-cell thickness swings between 0.1 and 1.12; the implicit vertical solves in model/src/solve_tridiagonal.F over 90 levels; and pkg/obcs with its sponge relaxation on the northern boundary..

Runtime knobs (`run.sh --help`): `SAB_STEPS` (default 80, the graded
value; the upstream deck runs 20 steps of 300 s) scales the
run linearly, and `SAB_BUILD_JOBS` (default 4) only the build. Expected run
time on the declared resources, build excluded: about 9 s;
the per-check build (roughly 40 s on an x86_64 host) is reported by `run.sh`
as `SAB_BUILD_SECONDS` and does not count against the suite budget.

## The two initial conditions

`ic/nominal` is the upstream deck, assembled as `testreport` assembles it
(the experiment's `input/`),
with these deck edits: `nTimeSteps` set to the graded window, `dumpFreq`,
`pChkptFreq` and `chkptFreq` set to zero and `dumpInitAndLast=.TRUE.` so that the only
state written is the initial and final dump, and
`writeBinaryPrec=64` so the dump is double precision and `useSingleCpuIO=.TRUE.` so the dump is one global file per field rather than one per tile; `SHELFICEwriteState=.TRUE.` in `data.shelfice`; `SHELFICE_dumpFreq=300.` in `data.shelfice`.

`ic/variant` holds only the deck files that differ, laid over `ic/nominal` by
`run.sh`; the difference is `SHELFICEDragQuadratic=0.0015000000000000005` in `data.shelfice` instead of 0.0015:
two ulps of the graded precision (binary64) on a parameter that enters the
tendency from the first step, a distinct double, so the two runs differ at
round-off level from the first step. The
spread between them is the check's measured sensitivity under the pass policy
and must stay inside the bound.

`run.sh altbuild` runs `ic/nominal` on an alternative build of the same source, `genmake2 -ieee` (gfortran -O0
-ffloat-store, strict IEEE arithmetic) instead of the optimised optfile; grading never uses it, self-validation measures the
check's floor between two legitimate builds from it.

## The pass policy

Every cell of every prognostic field in the final state dump must satisfy
|candidate - reference| <= 1e-10 + 1e-08 |reference|; inside `surfDiag` the records `MXLDEPTH`, `dynDiag` the records `CONVADJ` are not graded (MXLDEPTH is a level search that jumps by a whole level and CONVADJ counts discrete convection events).
The relative part is the working bound because the fields span many orders of
magnitude; the absolute part covers cells at or near zero. The observable is the end-of-run state dump (U, V, W, T, S, Eta, PH, PHL) plus the package's own shelfIceFreshWaterFlux and shelfIceHeatFlux fields, and the bound is physical because remeshing is a conservative rearrangement of a fixed amount of heat, salt and volume: the redistribution weights are determined by the grid, not tuned, so any error in them shows up as a non-conservation that the very next graded T and S field records at the percent level, and any error in the threshold test or in the order of the mass stepping moves an event by a whole step, which is an order-one difference. It is achievable, and the reason this deck is safe despite being built entirely around a threshold, is that the events are not marginal: the upstream log records the merges firing at top-cell thickness factors of 0.09888 and 0.09924 against a threshold of 0.10 and the splits at 1.121 and 1.208 against 1.12, so the crossing overshoots by about one part in a thousand in each case, thirteen orders of magnitude away from anything a one-ulp perturbation could flip. That margin is structural rather than lucky: the prescribed mass tendency changes the ice draft by roughly 0.0094 m per step against a 10 m level, so between two remeshing tests the thickness factor moves by about two parts in a thousand and can never sit within round-off of a threshold. The rest of the floor is set by cg2d, which stops on a global-sum residual test at 1e-11 after about 115 iterations, and by the implicit tridiagonal solves, which are exact to round-off. The window is 6.7 hours of a forced two-dimensional cavity with no chaotic mode, so the comparison stays pointwise; the measured spread is what the bound is finalised against.
Faults: Getting the redistribution wrong when the top cell splits or merges in pkg/shelfice/shelfice_remesh_state.F, for instance weighting the donor and receiver cells by thickness instead of by hFac times drF, breaks heat and salt conservation and moves theta and salt in the top few levels by parts in 1e-2 the first time an event fires. Forgetting to recompute the vertical velocity consistently in shelfice_remesh_calc_w.F, or failing to update the C, U and V masks in shelfice_remesh_c_mask.F and shelfice_remesh_uv_mask.F, leaves a stale mask and produces an order-one error in W and in the cell just below the ice. Shifting the merge or split threshold, or evaluating the thickness factor before rather than after the ice mass is stepped in shelfice_step_icemass.F, changes which step an event fires on, which the graded fields see as an order-one difference. On the melt side, dropping the quadratic top drag or the SHELFICEselectDragQuadr=2 form of the friction velocity moves SHIfwFlx by percents. A relaxed cg2d target of 1e-7 instead of 1e-11 moves Eta by parts in 1e-7, and single precision anywhere by parts in 1e-7.

## Evidence

Hazards. (1) The deck restarts from pickup.0000002898 and pickup_shelfice.0000002898, both of which must be copied and kept; nIter0=2898, so at 80 steps the graded files are named 0000002978. (2) The deck already sets dumpAtLast=.TRUE. in DIAGNOSTICS_LIST, so its two active diagnostics streams do land at the final iteration, and both are graded except for one record each, which is excluded: surfDiag's MXLDEPTH, which model/src/calc_oce_mxlayer.F computes by a level search with a stratification threshold and which therefore jumps by a whole 10 m level when the test flips, and dynDiag's CONVADJ, a discrete convection-event counter; in a near-neutral cavity column both are genuine flip risks under a one-ulp perturbation and neither is meaningful under a relative tolerance. Excluding those two records costs the direct melt-rate observable, which is why SHELFICEwriteState and SHELFICE_dumpFreq are turned on instead: they give SHICE_fwFlux and SHICE_heatFlux at every step, including the last, with no threshold in them. If a later calibration shows MXLDEPTH is in fact stable here, that record can be graded too and those two edits dropped. (3) The third and fourth diagnostics lists in data.diagnostics have no fileName and are silently ignored by pkg/diagnostics/diagnostics_readparms.F, which the upstream output.txt confirms by creating only surfDiag and dynDiag; in any case their frequency of -300 is a snapshot, and snapshot streams are written with the iteration number one below the current one, so they could never land on the final iteration. (4) The deck sets dumpFreq=10800 and pChkptFreq=chkptFreq=2592000; the generator zeroes all three, which also zeroes SHELFICE_dumpFreq because it defaults to dumpFreq, hence the explicit edit. (5) The northern open-boundary files vVel_obc.bin, temp_obc.bin and salt_obc.bin hold exactly one record each (90 doubles), so they are steady and put no cap on the window length; useOBCSprescribe with one record simply reuses it. (6) The ice draft moves only about 0.75 m over 80 steps against a 900 m water column and 90 levels, so there is no risk of running out of vertical levels or of grounding the shelf in this window. (7) readBinaryPrec=64 and writeBinaryPrec=64 are already set and all binaries are 64-bit; useSingleCpuIO=.TRUE. is harmless in a single-process run. (8) SHELFICEDragQuadratic is set explicitly by the deck to 0.0015 and is used by the SHELFICEselectDragQuadr=2 quadratic top-drag path (applied implicitly, selectImplicitDrag=2 in data) from the first step, feeding the friction velocity and therefore the transfer coefficients, so a two-ulp change touches both momentum and melt immediately. (9) data.pkg has no useMNC, so no NetCDF edit is needed.

Floor: self-validation measures it on every run from `run.sh altbuild`, the same source under genmake2 -ieee, graded against the nominal run with this check's validate.py, and records it in the rubric's evidence (floor, altbuild); that in-image number is the floor a reviewer reads. The native measurement of 2026-09-02 between the same two builds on the x86_64 host: the optimised gfortran build and the IEEE -O0 build are bit-identical on this deck over all 291400 graded values (floor 0.0, in no field; re-verified on 2026-09-04 with validate.py from the retained runs); the two builds pass each other under the rule. Faults, same build with one parameter changed: the cg2d target residual loosened to 1e-3 (from the deck's 1e-11) aborts the run at iteration 2959 of 2978, where MON_SOLUTION stops on an extreme potential temperature (theta_max 7.6e2 K, after hFacC < hFacInf warnings from iteration 2906), so no final dump exists to compare (FAIL; the deck diff, log tail and STDERR are retained under comment/fault-probes/shelfice-remesh), and the variant parameter off by five percent 2.2e+05 of the bound (FAIL). Measured run time of the nominal deck, build excluded: 8.4 s natively.
