**坑(3 条)**
  ! INSTRUMENT SET. The three word backends are x86-only (-mavx2 / -msse2) and there is no NEON path, and CMakeLists.txt:25 tests CMAKE_SYSTEM_PROCESSOR against a case-sensitive 'ARM64' that macOS's 'arm64' does not match, so on the packaging machine no machine flag is set at all and the portable bitword_64.h is what builds. Upstream's own reference times in stim_perf are 1.5-4x faster than this machine on the bit-parallel benchmarks. The incumbent must declare SIMD_WIDTH and host ISA explicitly or a port will be scored against a baseline 1.5-4x slower than Stim at speed.
  ! Sampling is stochastic: shot outcomes depend on an RNG stream, so any check that samples must either seed and accept that a port's stream will differ, or grade statistical invariants. Deterministic sub-paths exist and are preferable where available - tableau algebra, stabilizers_to_tableau, and reference sampling are all deterministic.
  ! simd_bits_not_zero_100K reports 310 ps against a 32 ns upstream reference, which is not a credible hundredfold speedup and suggests the compiler elides the loop. Do not build a graded workload on that benchmark without checking what it executes.

## bit-table-transpose

- 官方测试源:`code/stim/src/stim/mem/`
- 活配置(决定哪些分支被编译/执行):Dimension-512 Clifford tableau built deterministically from seed 409, inverted once and twice.
